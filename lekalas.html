LEKALAS

A template-based journey of your next sewing project.

Tools Figma
Timeline 2024-2025
Format Website, accessible to mobile users

Executive summary

The website inspires users to choose their next sewing project and makes it attainable. Accessibility is the most vital point of the idea. This leads to having: a visionary home page, an imaginative and structured library, helpful filtering, with the attention to size inclusivity.

The idea is well suited for the website format, accessible to mobile users, not necessarily app-based. Initially, I had not considered that it may have not been comfortable to develop an app where you could overlook a template, the screen is too small for judging its qualities, and modifying it would have been even harder. Consulting with lecturers helped me to decide on the website’s format and observing similar sites cemented my decision.

The main purpose of the website is to inspire users to start or continue their sewing journey and to provide help along the way.

How might we

Showcase

How might we showcase templates in an engaging way and keep the user immersed in their sewing journey?

Time

How might we measure the time and its dependence on the sewing level it would take for certain clothes to be made?

Sizing

How might we implement the tools for users to adjust the templates digitally?

Instructions

How might we ensure an easy navigation to the sewing process and provide clear instructions?

Methodology

3interviews, with people of different knowledge levels of sewing and social media
20‑40minutes per interview
Onlineall three main and final interviews, in the Lithuanian language

The method I used consisted of interviewing three people, possessing different knowledge levels of sewing and social media. The first interview I took was in person, but not having used a detailed interview guide, it was not as successful. It had to be redone because the amount of information gathered didn’t answer a lot of questions. I chose to do video calls to note the person better and present myself in a welcoming way for the participant. The audio was recorded with my phone using a voice memory application.

Trends & desk research

Fast fashion

Fast fashion cycles repress creativity and originality and trap people in the ever-changing need to buy clothes. The idea of sewing opposes every point listed above. It encourages creativity, makes the consideration of materials important, and puts value on labor.

Gen Z

People of this generation love individuality but surrender themselves to social media trends too. It is important to keep the design fresh and current.

Sizing

Plus size fashion is undervalued in the world fashion scheme, most stores lack inclusion. The website’s concept will highlight the option for users of different measurements to look up or modify templates to fit their sizes.

Key findings and recommendations

High

Categories

Make categories clear and easy to navigate. Must include as much specification as possible.

Difficulty

Determine the template’s difficulty and attach it to its specifications.

Sizing

Include sizing options, preferably a digital, interactive model a user could use to modify the pattern’s lines, which indicates different sizes.

Medium

Design

Habituate the user by implementing a familiar design.

Instructions

Add an option to include step-by-step instructions, but not make it mandatory. Keep it short and clear.

Pricing

Make the pricing visible when the user is looking through the selection.

File format

Include two file options, A4 or 1:1 scale. Do not limit downloads to A4.

Low

Navigation

Make the navigation visible and easy to locate. Make it a hamburger menu instead of permanently placed.

Showcase of the patterns

Place the full showcase template on a different page after it is selected. If it’s priced or protected by legal rights, replace the template with the drawn version of the clothing.

Time consumption

Do not pay attention to time consumption criteria as it can not be measured correctly and too dependable on the individual.

Sizing

At the bottom of the page there’s an interactive model of the template you can modify when subtracting and extending notches and lines. The result: selectable sewing notches transforming the sizing lines when moved.

Personal reflection

This research greatly expanded my knowledge of UI/UX design and structure occurrences. Diving into research, I did not expect my previous thoughts to be subverted as they were, which helped me to choose wisely ideas I would later pursue and reject impossible proposals.

One of the participants I had found fit my persona absolutely perfectly. It was extremely resourceful to hear a person’s thoughts I had vividly imagined as my protopersona. Interviews had taken place earlier than the prototype was finished, leading to a lot of ideas being described, not that much shown visually. The first interview I took was not as structured as the later ones, it showed me how important it is to have an interview guide.

Interview guide
  • Introduction: greeting, purpose of the interview, duration, confidentiality.
  • Warm-up questions about their personal life and the ability to balance different activities
  • Elaboration of the navigation
  • Application comparison
  • Size preferences and clothing preferences
  • Processing their knowledge of templates
  • Usage of sewing tools and knowledge of sewing machines
  • Estimation of the difficulty levels
  • Filtering section
  • Time management and preparation
  • Usage of materials
  • Discussing their stitching preferences and the importance of it
  • Community and engagement
  • Conversing about the upload process of the templates
  • Sizing
  • Interface
  • The end: “What’s your final impression of the idea”.
