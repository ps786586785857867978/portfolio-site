Executive summary

DHL care is an application, created to address a growing gap in the logistics market: the lack of widely accessible, care focused delivery solutions for fragile, high value (monetary, sentimentally) items of significance. The service combines: white glove couriers, packing one’s order onsite, live sensor, monitoring: temperature, humidity, shockwaves. All this visualised through digital app interface. The application provides live insights into parcel’s journey from order being placed to delivered.

White glove handling

A special courier is assigned to handle delicate parcels with on site packing and handover.

Live condition monitoring

“IoT” tracker showing parcel’s live location. Allows to monitor temperature, humidity, shockwaves.

Premium digital experience

An app, created to visualise all these elements and guide the user throughout parcel’s journey.

Vision

To redefine widespread logistics by making care, transparency, responsibility as visible and valuable as speed. To reshape customer perceptions of what mass logistics can deliver.

Mission

The mission is to create a digital product, providing individuals with contentment when shipping items of significance. Done by combining professional human handling with insightful, live shipment monitoring.

Brand proposition

DHL operates at a very fast pace, focusing on maximizing efficiency and profits, which is reflected in their mission and vision. My proposed idea seeks to subvert this approach by focusing on slow, precise, and careful handling, positioning the company as a trusted provider for fragile, high-value, or sentimentally important arts and craft items.

Gap in the market

No globally accessible premium service that combines careful handling, live digital tracking, and emotional reassurance for shippers. DHL Care leverages DHL’s global network, filling this gap for a market that wants white glove quality but cannot access extremely expensive services.

Target customer segments

High value personal shippers

Adults aged 18-65, middle to high income. Occasionally or frequently ship fragile, valuable, or sentimental items. Needs: emotional reassurance, careful handling, visibility of parcel condition, accountability.

Artisans, craftsmen, creatives

Independent artists, crafters, creators in general shipping delicate products to clients worldwide. Needs: reliable handling, digital tracking for transparency, global access for shipping.

Occasional sentimental shippers

Individuals sending emotionally important items, like family heirlooms, gifts, perhaps less frequent shippers but highly sensitive towards risk. Needs: maximum reassurance, tracking, safe handling.

Value creation

Products & services

White glove couriers; sensor, live tracking; DHL Care app.

Customer jobs

To ship fragile, valuable, or sentimental items safely; monitor shipment; reduce stress during transit.

Pains

Gains

Peace; transparency and control over shipments; assurance of safe delivery; ability to track environmental conditions in real time.

Revenue model

DHL Care monetises the value it creates through a standardised premium fee per shipment, added on top of existing DHL pricing. The fee is based only on parcel weight and dimensions, not on the declared value of the contents.

Customers pay for how the parcel is treated, not what it is worth. This includes white glove handling, professional packing, reduced automated processing, live sensor based monitoring (location, temperature, humidity, shock).
