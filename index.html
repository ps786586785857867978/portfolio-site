Cultural Alleys

Cultural Alleys is a non-profit organisation based in Lithuania, which operates at the intersection of artistic practice, academic inquiry, and community engagement across various disciplines. I am its Project Manager and Design Architect.

I created the organisation's complete brand and visual system. Coded the website and currently maintain it. Made the organisation's loading animation and the illustrations for the website. Additionally, I regularly create poster designs for organisation’s activities, anouncements, features. Last year, I helped to curate the digital gallery project "Fragments of Becoming" as well as coded and designed its interactive flipbook.
